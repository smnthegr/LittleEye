## Inclusion and display rules

- Include all fully specified alternatives found for relevant city/provincial dispatch, medical assistance, ambulance, rescue, Red Cross, and hospitals listed in emergency directories. Keep source-specific network labels ahead of offline brand estimates.
- Preserve department and barangay names. Province-wide services are labelled as provincial, and appear only within their province. The existing picker covers cities; municipality-specific services are not assigned to a nearby city.
- Generic hospital contacts are labelled as hospital contacts, not guaranteed direct ER/ambulance dispatch. National 911/143 appear ahead of hospital switchboards. Do not invent 24/7 hours or pediatric capabilities.
- Exclude unrelated admin/personal/fax/text-only/utility, health-office lines outside emergency listings, COVID-only, police-only and fire-only contacts. Health offices explicitly offered for emergency assistance remain labelled as medical assistance after dispatch options. Include BFP when reviewed official sources document its medical/search/rescue role; Bayugan’s Smart and Globe alternatives therefore both remain.
- Extensions are shown separately; Call opens the published base phone number and the user requests the extension. No concatenated digits or automated DTMF.
- Withhold ambiguous/obsolete local dialing notation instead of inventing subscriber digits. Contextual regional area codes are used for fully specified local subscriber numbers; no missing subscriber digits are filled.
